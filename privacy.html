We're a family business that has been lighting South African homes since 1974. When you shop with us you trust us with your name, your address and sometimes photos of your home. This policy explains, in plain language, what we do with that information and the choices you have, as required by the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

01Who we are

The Lighting Warehouse ("TLW", "we", "us") is the responsible party for personal information collected through this website, our 12 stores, our WhatsApp line and our trade portal. Our Information Officer oversees how we handle personal information and can be reached at info@lightingwarehouse.co.za or on 011 201 2600.

02What we collect

  • Contact details: your name, email address and mobile number.
  • Order details: delivery or collection address, the products you buy, and your order history.
  • Payment information: card payments are processed by our payment partners. We do not see or store your full card number. For EFT we keep the payment reference.
  • Trade applications: company name, VAT number, trade type and estimated volume.
  • Things you share with us: messages, photos of damaged items, and room photos you upload to our AI tools.
  • Website usage: pages viewed, device and browser type, and approximate location, through cookies (see section 05).

03Why we use it

We only use your information for a clear reason, and only as much as we need:

  • To process, deliver and support your orders, returns and courier collections.
  • To send order updates by email and WhatsApp, which are part of the service you asked for.
  • To answer your questions and give product advice.
  • To assess trade applications and manage trade accounts.
  • To prevent fraud and meet our legal and tax obligations.
  • To send marketing, but only if you've agreed or you're an existing customer who hasn't opted out (section 06).
We will never sell your personal information. Not to anyone, not for any price.

04Who we share it with

We share only what each partner needs to do their job, under written agreements that require them to protect it:

  • Courier companies, to deliver and collect parcels.
  • Payment providers (card processing and Payflex), to take payment securely.
  • IT and hosting providers, who run our website, email and WhatsApp services. Some may store data outside South Africa, in which case we make sure the protection is at least as strong as POPIA requires.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

06Marketing

We'd love to tell you about Black Friday, new ranges and the summer fan sale, but only if you want to hear from us. Every email and WhatsApp message has a way to opt out, and you can change your preferences in your account at any time. Opting out of marketing never affects order updates.

07Keeping it safe

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), restrict access to people who need it, and train our staff on handling personal information. We keep order records for as long as tax law requires, and other information only as long as we need it. If a breach ever affects your information, we will tell you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.

08Your rights

Under POPIA you may:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you, and for a copy.
  • Ask us to correct or delete it.
  • Object to us using it, including for direct marketing.
  • Withdraw consent you've given us.
  • Complain to the Information Regulator.

Email our Information Officer at info@lightingwarehouse.co.za. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request.

09Contact & complaints

Talk to us first. Most things are sorted with one email or call to info@lightingwarehouse.co.za or 011 201 2600. If you're not satisfied, you can lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa) through inforegulator.org.za.
