Still stuck?

Delivery

Do you deliver nationwide?

Yes. We deliver to every province in South Africa by tracked courier. Delivery is free on orders over R500. Most Gauteng orders arrive in 1 to 2 working days and other areas in 2 to 4 working days. You'll get email and WhatsApp updates at every step, and you can track your order without logging in.

How much does delivery cost?

Free on orders over R500. Below that, a flat courier fee is shown at checkout before you pay. Click & collect at any of our 12 stores is always free.

How are fragile items packed?

Fragile glass items are double-boxed: every glass shade and globe is wrapped, boxed and then boxed again before it leaves us.

Returns

How do returns work?

Returns and exchanges are collected from your door by courier. Send us a message or use Report a problem on the tracking page, and we'll book the collection and arrange a replacement, exchange or refund.

What if my order arrives damaged or incomplete?

Please keep the packaging and tell us as soon as possible with a photo, using Report a problem, WhatsApp or email. We'll collect the item by courier and send a replacement.

Payment

Which payment methods do you accept?

Card (Visa and Mastercard), EFT and Payflex, which splits your purchase into four interest-free payments every two weeks. Card payments are handled on our payment partner's secure page, so we never see your card number.

When does an EFT order ship?

EFT orders are packed as soon as the payment reflects in our account, which usually takes 1 to 2 working days depending on your bank.

Warranty

Do your products carry a warranty?

Yes. Products are covered by the supplier's warranty against manufacturing defects. The period depends on the product. Keep your invoice and contact us if something stops working, and we'll take it from there.

Installation advice

Can you install my lights or fan?

We advise on choosing, positioning and sizing your lighting in store, on WhatsApp and with our . Fixed lights and ceiling fans should be installed by a registered electrician, who can issue a Certificate of Compliance (CoC).

What size ceiling fan do I need?

As a guide, a fan of about 100 to 120cm suits a bedroom or study, and 130cm and up suits a lounge or open-plan space. Keep the blades at least 2.1m above the floor. Our lighting tools can work it out for your room.
