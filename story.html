Follow the cable
1973 London

A trade learned in a lampshade factory.

John spent 1973 in a lampshade factory in London, learning the craft from the frame up: how a shade is cut, stretched and stitched, and how the right shade can change the whole feeling of a room. It was a lesson about light that he brought home to Johannesburg.

1974 Fox Street, Johannesburg

Two rooms. Two employees. One customer.

The Lighting Warehouse opened in two rooms on Fox Street with two employees and, on that first day, one customer. Everyone who came through the door got the same thing they get today: someone who knew lighting, took the time to listen, and helped them find the right light.

1974 – 2002 The growing years

More rooms, more light.

Word spread the way it does in Joburg: from one happy customer to their neighbours, their builders and their friends. Two rooms became a warehouse, then showrooms across Gauteng. The range grew from shades to pendants, chandeliers, fans, outdoor lights and globes, sourced from local makers and from suppliers around the world.

2003 The light carries on

The family carries the light forward.

In January 2003 John passed away. His wife, son, daughter and nephew stepped forward, together with the staff he had always treated as family, and kept the business growing in the way he built it: a wide range, honest advice and service that people come back for.

Today South Africa

South Africa's leading independent lighting retailer.

Twelve showrooms, from Roodepoort to our newest store in uMhlanga, our first in KwaZulu-Natal. One of the widest and trendiest ranges in the country, delivery nationwide (free over R500) and load-shedding solutions for every home. Still family-run. Still answering the phone ourselves.
